Island Cove

Logo Design • Branding • 2026

A restaurant logo for a coastal dining concept.The mark is a fish with a palm tree cut into the body. I chose organic edges to keep it feeling coastal and handmade rather than overworked. Coral, cream, and warm brown. The wordmark sits inside the curve of the tail so the whole thing reads as one unit.
