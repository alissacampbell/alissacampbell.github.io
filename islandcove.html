Island Cove

Logo Design • Branding • 2026

A restaurant logo concept built around a fish silhouette with a palm tree cut into the body — a double-read mark that communicates tropical coastal dining without spelling it out. The coral, cream, and warm brown palette keeps the brand feeling relaxed but premium. The wordmark was composed to sit within the tail's curve, unifying the mark and type into a single cohesive shape.
