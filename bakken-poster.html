Bakken Museum Poster

Graphic Design • 2026

A class assignment to visit a museum and design a poster for one of its exhibits. I chose the Bakken Museum in Minneapolis and their Frankenstein's Laboratory experience — a 10-minute immersive walk-through included with admission. The poster leans into classic horror movie aesthetics: a deep monochromatic red palette, jagged display typography, and a painted illustration style referencing vintage sci-fi and creature feature posters. The silhouetted figure, glowing eyes, and lightning-veined creature build atmosphere without giving too much away.
