The Japanese House

Graphic Design • 2026

A class assignment to design a concert poster for a real artist. I chose The Japanese House and wanted the poster to communicate what her music actually feels like, dreamy, hazy, a little psychedelic. The mixed type styles were pulled from 70s rock posters, which felt like the right reference for the weight her voice carries.
