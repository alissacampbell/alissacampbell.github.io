The Japanese House

Graphic Design • 2026

A class assignment to design a concert poster for a fictional show. I chose The Japanese House, a British indie artist known for her dreamy, atmospheric sound. The poster pulls from the visual language of 70s rock and Britpop — retro bubble lettering, a film-grain texture, and a warm stage photo blown out with purple and amber light. The fictional bill pairs her with Blur and Oasis to lean further into that era. Show details are set at First Avenue in Minneapolis, keeping it grounded in a real venue.
