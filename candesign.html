Dirty Martini — Drinks Co.

Packaging • 2026

A class assignment where every student designed a can for the fictional brand Drinks Co. I chose a Dirty Martini flavor, pairing a loose script with a tight serif and a red, cream, and teal palette to keep it feeling premium. The abstract illustrations and scalloped label edge treat the design as a physical object rather than just a flat graphic.
