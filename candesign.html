Dirty Martini — Drinks Co.

Packaging • 2026

A class assignment where every student was given the same brief: design a drink can for the fictional brand Drinks Co. I chose a Dirty Martini flavor and built the label around a red, cream, and teal palette with gold accents that carry through to the can itself. The typography pairs a loose script for "Dirty" against a tight serif for "MARTINI" — the contrast keeps it feeling premium without being stiff. Abstract illustrations of a martini glass and citrus slices fill the lower half, loose enough to feel editorial rather than decorative. The scalloped edge at the top of the label treats the design as a physical object, not just a flat graphic.
